Six small towns, one December weekend

The first weekend of December is when New England stops being about leaves and starts looking like Christmas. These are the six towns on the shortlist, with Chatham, a week later, as the outside option. Pick one to see the pros and cons, where to stay, and things to do.

    Compare all six

    Where the weekends fall

    Most of these towns hold their holiday events on the first weekend of December (Dec 4 to 6). Chatham, the outside option, follows on Dec 11 to 13.

    What I could not confirm

    • Exact 2026 dates for Wickford’s Festival of Lights and Lenox’s Winterland. Both ran on the first weekend of December in earlier years.
    • The Wainwright Inn in Great Barrington appeared in a real-estate listing at some point. Check that it is open.
    • Lenox’s Whitlock and Dewey inns changed names and management in 2022, so older reviews may describe different places.
    • Wickford has little lodging in the village. The Hamilton Village Inn information comes from a 2022 article.
    • Winter hours for restaurants in Kennebunkport and Chatham. Call before you plan a dinner around any one place.
    • Prices. Rates shown in sources were from other seasons, so I left them out or marked them as approximate.
